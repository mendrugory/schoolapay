## Introduction

SchoolaPay is designed to help schools manage activity payments, parent coordination, QR ticketing, receipts, reminders, and attendance validation. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information submitted through our website and demo request forms.

## Information We Collect

- School name
- Contact name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Role at school
- Number of students
- School website, if provided
- Current payment or administration challenges
- Basic technical information such as browser, device, and website usage data when analytics are enabled

## How We Use Information

- Responding to demo requests
- Understanding school requirements
- Improving the website and product
- Communicating about SchoolaPay
- Supporting onboarding or pilot discussions

## Student and Parent Data

SchoolaPay may process parent, student, activity, receipt, ticketing, and payment-related information when used by a school. The specific data handled depends on school configuration, selected payment provider, and implementation scope.

## Payment Information

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. SchoolaPay does not intend to store full payment card details on its own systems unless explicitly implemented with appropriate provider controls.

## Data Sharing

- Service providers required for website, email, hosting, analytics, and demo request workflows
- Payment providers where applicable
- Legal or operational requirements when necessary
- SchoolaPay does not sell personal information

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls and secure workflow practices.

## Data Retention

We keep demo request information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the relationship, improve the product, or meet operational requirements.

## Your Choices

- Contact us to request updates or deletion of demo request information
- Opt out of non-essential communications where applicable

## Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SchoolaPay develops and as services expand.
