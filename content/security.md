## Our Security Approach

SchoolaPay is designed around secure workflows for schools, parents, activity payments, receipts, QR tickets, and attendance validation.

## Controlled Access

- Role-based access for school staff
- Permission-aware workflows
- Separation between parent and staff experiences

## Clear Records and Auditability

- Payment status records
- Receipt history
- Activity records
- Ticket validation records
- Attendance scan visibility

## Payment Processing

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. The final payment security model depends on the selected provider and implementation scope.

## Data Handling

- Parent and student information should be handled responsibly
- Access should be limited to authorized users
- Data collected should support school activity workflows

## Operational Reliability

Where configured, offline fallback workflows may support event validation during unstable connectivity.

## Responsible Development

- Secure-by-design development practices
- Review of access and workflow controls
- Ongoing improvements as pilots and product development progress

## Report a Security Concern

Contact [hello@schoolapay.com](mailto:hello@schoolapay.com) to report a security concern.

Formal security certifications and compliance statements will be published only when verified.
